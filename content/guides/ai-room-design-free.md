You can try AI room design free on your iPhone with Room Redo. Take one photo of your own room, pick a style, and the app redesigns that same room in the new look. Your first redesign is free: the full redesign, in any style you like, with a small Room Redo watermark. There's no account to make, no email, no password.

Below is one real room, a narrow spare room nobody wanted to use, redesigned three ways by the app. Then the plain details: how it works, what's free, what's paid, and the questions people search before they try.

## Same room, three looks

Here's the starting point. A long, thin spare room with scuffed white walls, a bare bulb, one window at the far end, a radiator under it and an ironing board leaning on the wall. It's the kind of room that collects boxes.

Drag the slider to see it in Scandinavian style.

That's the point of trying more than one style. You often don't know what a room wants to be until you see it as three different rooms.

## How AI room design works in Room Redo

Three steps, all on your iPhone.

1. **Take a photo of your room.** Use the camera or pick one from your photo library.
2. **Pick a style.** Choose a Serious style or a Wild idea.
3. **See your room redesigned.** The app shows the redesign developing as it happens. When it's done, drag between before and after.

You can also ask for up to three things you'd like in the room, such as plants or a rug. Want another look? Try more styles on the same room. Every room you redesign is kept in "My spaces", so you can come back to it.

The difference from a stock gallery is the starting point. Room Redo starts from your photo, not a stock room, so the redesign is of your room, not a picture of somebody else's.

## Pick from more than 45 styles

The styles come in two kinds.

**Serious** styles are real interior styles you could furnish a room in: Japandi, Scandinavian, Soft Parisian, Mid-Century Modern, Art Deco and more. These are the ones to use when you're planning paint, a rug or a new bed.

**Wild** ideas are for fun and for big what-ifs: a spa bath, a cat lounge, a library nook, a coffee and matcha bar, a starry sky ceiling. They rebuild the room around one idea, like the reading nook above.

## Rooms and corners that work

Room Redo works on bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens, bathrooms, offices and balconies.

You don't have to redesign a whole room, either. Photograph a single corner and say what it should do. There are six purposes to choose from: storage, a place to sit, work or study, laundry, a space for kids, or a space for your pet. You get an idea for that exact spot, which is handy for the awkward corner by the stairs or the dead space next to a closet.

## Is this AI room design free, really?

Yes: your first redesign is free, one per device. It isn't a cut-down preview. You get the full redesign, in any style, including the Wild ones. It carries a small Room Redo watermark, which stays until the device has bought something.

That makes it a fair test. Pick the style you're most curious about, use your real room, and judge the result before you spend anything.

## Do I need to sign up or log in?

No. There's no sign-up, no email and no password. Your device is enough.

The app creates a private ID on your device to keep track of your redesigns and purchases. Your photos are stored so your rooms stay in My spaces, and you can delete your data from Settings in the app. The [privacy policy](/privacy) has the details.

## Can I use a photo of my own room?

Yes, that's the whole idea. Room Redo redesigns a photo of your own room, taken with your camera or picked from your library.

The one rule: photograph the space without people in it. Photos with people in them are turned away. Stand in the doorway or a corner so the shot takes in as much of the room as you can, like the spare room above.
