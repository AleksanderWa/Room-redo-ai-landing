## Intent

The searcher wants to see their own room redesigned by AI without paying, and often without signing up. Many expect a website tool. We win the ones on an iPhone, or the ones willing to grab their iPhone. They install when they believe the result will be *their* room, it's genuinely free to try, and there's no account wall.

## SERP now

- All small AI-tool sites: archivinci.com, decai.ai, decoratly.com, styly.io, roomgpt.io, reimaginehome.ai, floor-plan.ai, paintit.ai, plus one YouTube video.
- They're tool homepages with thin copy, feature lists and "free" that often turns out to mean credits or a login wall.
- No one shows a clear, real before/after story of one room.

## Angle

"Redesign your room with AI, free, on your iPhone. No account." Show one real small room (the boxroom) redesigned three ways by the app. Explain exactly what the free redesign is: the full redesign, any style, one per device, with a small watermark. Also say what you get if you want more. The confidence comes from being specific, and the honesty about "free" is itself the selling point, because the competitors are vague. Never mention or link competitors.

## Must include

- First screen and first 100 words: iPhone app, first redesign free, no account or sign-up. Primary keyword in the first sentence or two.
- The boxroom before/after with the slider (Scandinavian), then the same room in Mid-Century Modern and Cozy Lounge Nook. "Same room, three looks." Mention that Wild styles like Cozy Lounge Nook rebuild the room around an idea, so the changes are bigger.
- How it works in 3 steps: photo (camera or library), pick a style, see your room.
- Style showcase: Serious and Wild, with a `::style-card` row: japandi, scandinavian, cozy_cat_lounge, coffee_matcha_bar. Use "more than 45 styles", never an exact count.
- Rooms that work, including corners (FACTS.md).
- "What's free and what's paid": a short table with the plans and packs exactly as in FACTS.md.
- Question H2s that match searches, each answered in the first sentence:
  - "Is it really free?"
  - "Do I need to sign up or log in?"
  - "Can I use a photo of my own room?"
  - "Does it work on Android or in a browser?" Answer in one sentence, positively: "Room Redo is an iPhone app; the web page is where you start." Don't dwell.
- `::cta` mid-page and at the end.

## Must avoid

- Any competitor name or link. Any speed claim. Any exact style count. Preservation promises (the images show the door, window and radiator stayed, but don't promise it). Provider names. Calling outputs "renders" or "AI art".
- External links aren't required for this piece. Add one only if it backs a specific claim, and never to a competitor.
