## Intent

The searcher likes the Japandi look (calm, warm wood, low furniture, few things) and wants to know how to get it in *their* bedroom or living room: what to change, what to buy, what to remove. They install when they see a real, ordinary room (messy, rented, odd ceiling) become convincingly Japandi, and realise they can see their own room that way before buying anything.

## Value first (updated 2026-10-21, vault "2026-10-21 SEO Step 4 - Growth Loop"; STYLE.md section 1 wins over anything below)

reader takeaway: the reader can turn their own bedroom or living room Japandi without the app. They know the palette (named colours), the materials, the furniture rules (low, few, natural), what to remove first, cheap swaps, and the mistakes that make it look cold or themed.

value sections (each must pass the remove-the-app test; the first H2 is one of these, pure value):
1. What Japandi style is, and its rules: Japanese and Scandinavian roots in 2 to 3 sourced sentences. Then the rules as a scannable list: warm minimalism, natural materials (light oak or ash plus a few darker wood or black accents; linen, cotton, wool, paper, ceramic, stone), low furniture, open floor, closed storage, handmade and imperfect things, a few plants.
2. "What colours are Japandi?" Answer in the first sentence. A palette table: warm white, greige, sand, clay or terracotta, muted sage, charcoal or black accents, each with where it goes (walls, textiles, accents). Explain why warm undertones beat cool grey.
3. Japandi bedroom: layout and rules. Low platform bed, a simple nightstand or none, hidden storage, layered linen bedding in 2 to 3 tones, warm low light (paper lantern, warm-white bulbs), one artwork, nothing on the floor. **The app demo goes here:** the attic bedroom slider, with what changed tied to each rule.
4. Japandi living room: layout and rules. Low sofa in linen or boucle, a solid wood coffee table, poufs instead of extra chairs, one large sculptural plant, sheer curtains, a rug that defines the seating zone, closed storage. **App demo:** the old-town living room slider.
5. "Is Japandi the same as Scandinavian?" A comparison table (palette, wood tone, furniture height, pattern, mood). The same living room in Japandi vs Scandinavian is the visual proof.
6. "Can I make a small bedroom Japandi?" Yes, with practical small-room rules (low furniture keeps sightlines, under-bed storage, one wall colour, mirror placement).
7. Start this weekend: what to remove first, then low-cost swaps in priority order (declutter surfaces, swap to linen bedding, warm bulbs, a paper lantern, one plant, and paint last). Use `:::steps` or a checklist.
8. Mistakes that ruin Japandi: too cold or grey, all-matching blond wood, too much decor, Asian-themed kitsch, harsh overhead light, everything bright white.

where the app fits: sections 3, 4 and 5. The app shows each rule applied to a real, ordinary room (messy attic bedroom, half-renovated living room).
